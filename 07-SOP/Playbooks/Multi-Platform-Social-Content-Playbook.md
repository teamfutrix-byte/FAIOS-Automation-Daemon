## 🔄 5-Step Approval & 7-Day Advance Scheduling Workflow

1. **AI CMO Content Generation**:
   - `emp_script_writer` generates Master Prompt v14.1 educational documentary scripts.
   - `emp_insta_mgr` renders 9:16 videos on **Google Flow Omini** (locked Founder Avatar) and designs 1:1 / 16:9 graphic banners & carousels.
2. **AI CEO Quality Audit**:
   - Verifies 0% factual error in NEET/JEE formulas and WCAG Mute-Test visual compliance.
3. **Telegram Approval Intercept**:
   - Dispatches a multi-platform content card to `@FutrixCEOAIbot` with full platform previews, captions, and media download links.
4. **Founder Telegram Gate Approval**:
   - Founder reviews preview card and taps `[ ✅ APPROVE & SCHEDULE (7-DAY BUFFER) ]`.
5. **Automated 7-Day Advance Multi-Platform Scheduling**:
   - Upon receiving approval, AI CMO automatically schedules posts 7 days in advance in Google Sheets `Scheduled_Posts` tab mapped for Instagram, YouTube Shorts, Facebook Page, and X (Twitter).
