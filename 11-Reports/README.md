# 11-Reports — Decision Intelligence, Business Analytics & Reports

## 📌 Purpose
The `11-Reports` module is the Decision Brain of FUTRIX. It transforms raw telemetry data from Supabase Postgres, Google Gemini API, n8n, and GitHub Actions into actionable executive intelligence, predictive forecasts, early warning risk alerts, and concise Founder Mode decision recommendations.

## 👤 Owner & Scope
- **Owner**: AI CEO, AI CFO, & Chief Data Officer (`04-Departments/13-Data-Analytics`)
- **Scope**: Enterprise-wide business intelligence operating on a **95% AI Automation / 5% Founder Approval** model.

- **Downstream Dependencies**: Executive Decision Making & Founder Telegram Approval Gate.
