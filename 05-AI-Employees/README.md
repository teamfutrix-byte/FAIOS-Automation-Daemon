# 05-AI-Employees — Specialised Pedagogical & Technical AI Workers

## 📌 Purpose
The `05-AI-Employees` module organizes the 26 full-time AI Workers of FUTRIX. Every AI Employee belongs to exactly one department, reports to one AI Executive manager, reuses atomic tools from `06-Skills`, operates on a **95% AI Automation / 5% Founder Approval** model, and maintains dedicated documentation, prompts, and KPIs.

## 👤 Owner & Scope
- **Owner**: Department Leads & AI Executives (`03-Executives`)
- **Scope**: Platform-wide execution across Socratic tutoring, code development, video reel generation, doubt routing, security audits, and academic QA.

## 🔗 Dependencies
- **Upstream Dependencies**: `00-Codex`, `01-Blueprint`, `02-Frameworks`, `03-Executives`, `04-Departments`.
- **Downstream Dependencies**: `06-Skills`, `07-SOP`, `08-Automation`.
