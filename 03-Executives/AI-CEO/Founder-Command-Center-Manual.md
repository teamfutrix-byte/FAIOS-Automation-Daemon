## 📌 Founder Mode & Capacity Rules

The **Founder Command Center** is engineered to protect Founder focus, energy, and decision velocity. The AI CEO operates 95% of the company autonomously, transforming the Founder from an operational task-doer into a supreme strategic approver.

### Core Operating Principles for Founder Mode:
1. **4-Hour Capacity Lock**: System design assumes maximum 4 productive hours per day from the Founder.
2. **Top 3 Actions Only**: The AI CEO will NEVER present 50 pending tasks. Every daily brief distills company operations into the **Top 3 Highest-ROI Decisions**.
3. **Telegram Gate Interface**: All approvals (video reel releases, schema changes, pricing adjustments, category expansion) are presented via inline `[ ✅ APPROVE ]` / `[ ❌ REJECT ]` buttons on the Founder Telegram Bot.
4. **Motivation & Focus Protection**: The AI CEO continuously monitors sprint velocity, protecting the Founder from burnout during slow-result periods by surfacing quick-win student feedback and automated progress milestones.
