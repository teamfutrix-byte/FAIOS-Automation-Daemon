## 🏛 Executive Authority Tier Overview

All 15 C-Level AI Executives operate within strict, non-intersecting operational Tiers:

- **Tier 0: Founder Sovereignty (Human-in-the-Loop)** — Telegram Approval Gate Required.
- **Tier 1: AI CEO Strategic Execution** — Cross-executive direction and Founder proposal assembly.
- **Tier 2: C-Suite Domain Ownership** — Departmental execution (CTO, CMO, COO, CFO, CPO, CAO, etc.).
- **Tier 3: Specialized Worker Execution** — AI Employees and skill bindings.
