# Privacy-First Knowledge & Data Policy

> **Document Type**: System Privacy Standard & Student Data Protection Policy  
> **Status**: Production Standard (v1.0.0)  

---

## 🔒 Core Data Privacy Principles

1. **Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP Act) Compliance**: All student interactions, phone numbers, and telemetry vectors are encrypted at rest (AES-256) in Supabase Postgres.
2. **Zero Third-Party Data Sales**: FUTRIX never sells, monetizes, or transfers student personal data to third-party advertisers or data brokers.
3. **Privacy-Safe Knowledge Graph**: Knowledge graph nodes store aggregated topic difficulty vectors and anonymized performance scores. Zero PII is exposed across public endpoints.
4. **Student Data Erasure SOP**: Students possess complete rights to request 100% data deletion or export via automated API endpoints (`/api/user/delete-data`).
