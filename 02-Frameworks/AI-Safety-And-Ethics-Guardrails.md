## 🛡 AI Safety & Ethical Directives

1. **Anti-Hallucination Mandate**: All pedagogical AI Tutors must solve mathematical and physical equations using verified formulas from `09-Knowledge`. If uncertain, the tutor MUST decline and escalate to `emp_qbank_auditor`.
