# Department 05 — Brand Marketing & Educational Reels

## 📌 Purpose
The Marketing department leads viral organic growth, educational documentary reel production (NEET/JEE focus), market trend research, permanent Founder avatar visual locking (Google Flow / Gemini / Omini), and advance social media scheduling for FUTRIX.

## 🎯 Mission
Build India's most recognized educational documentary series on Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts, driving millions of organic NEET and JEE student registrations through high-retention storytelling.

- **Reporting Executive**: AI CMO (Chief Marketing Officer)
- **Internal Customers**: Growth, Content Engineering, Design, AI CEO
- **External Customers**: NEET UG, NEET PG, JEE Main, JEE Advanced Aspirants
