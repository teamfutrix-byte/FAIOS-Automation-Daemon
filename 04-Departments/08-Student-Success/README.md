# Department 08 — Student Success & Doubt SLA Management

## 📌 Purpose
The Student Success department monitors student doubt resolution SLAs (<60 seconds), score trajectory analytics, personalized study plans, and onboarding support for NEET and JEE candidates.

## 🎯 Mission
Guarantee sub-60-second doubt resolution response times and drive average mock score improvements of +50 marks across all NEET/JEE candidates.

## 👤 Key Stakeholders & Structure
- **Owner**: Lead Student Success Agent
- **Reporting Executive**: AI CSSO (Chief Student Success Officer)
- **Internal Customers**: AI Tutor, Product, Academic Engineering
- **External Customers**: FUTRIX Student Aspirants
