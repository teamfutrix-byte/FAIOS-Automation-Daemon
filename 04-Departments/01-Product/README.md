# Department 01 — Product Management

## 📌 Purpose
The Product Management department defines feature roadmaps, student retention mechanics, mobile web app user experience, and sprint backlog prioritization for FUTRIX across NEET UG, NEET PG, JEE Main, and JEE Advanced.

## 🎯 Mission
Build India's most habit-forming, friction-free AI educational user experience that maximizes student learning velocity and long-term exam success.

## 👤 Key Stakeholders & Structure
- **Owner**: Lead Product Manager Agent
- **Reporting Executive**: AI CPO (Chief Product Officer)
- **Internal Customers**: Engineering, Design, Student Success, AI Tutor
- **External Customers**: NEET & JEE Student Candidates
